The problem

The box arrives “smashed”. Or does it?

Buyers photograph damage. Now an app can fake the photo in seconds. Sellers pay for breakage that never happened.

The tag

Put a witness inside the parcel.

A small tag rides in the box and feels every knock, drop and tilt. Each reading is signed by a key that never leaves the tag, so it can't be edited later.

The trip

Every hit is on the record.

Your limits depend on what you ship. Glass gets a low limit. Hardware gets a high one. Only readings inside the delivery window count.

The verdict

An investigator that only reads evidence.

SERV Reasoning reads the sensor record, never the buyer's story as fact. Its answer is checked against the evidence before you see it. You make the call.

The proof

Anyone can check it.

Every decision gets a public page with hashes of the evidence and the record. If a reading or the decision is changed later, the check fails.

Stop paying for damage that never happened.
